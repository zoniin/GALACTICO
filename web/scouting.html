How the comparison works

Each player’s rate uses reported events per 90 minutes with a known value. A recorded zero counts. Missing values and their minutes are excluded. Madrid’s reference pools players in the same provider position.

The opponent table uses completed league matches before the fixture’s UTC date, ordered by points, goal difference and goals scored. The top-six split starts once every club has played five games. Ties across sixth place stay unclassified. All opponents includes those games.

The table orders eligible players by the selected rate in their own league. It applies no league multiplier. Role, minutes, data coverage and transfer cost all matter when interpreting a gap.
