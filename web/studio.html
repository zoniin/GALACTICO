About these numbers
  • The proposed XI is picked by appearances this season.
  • Appearances and goals are this season’s, in all competitions, from the Wikipedia season article.
  • The next match comes from the schedule the Matches page reads: OpenFootball, and OpenLigaDB for the Champions League. Its kick-off is shown in your time zone.
  • An activity map is an archive sample: the player’s last recorded match in the public 2017/18 Wyscout data (Pappalardo et al.). A player with no sample shows no map. Colour shows where his actions started, not how they went.
  • A role area is drawn from the formation, not measured.
  • A link carries the formation and the shirt numbers. A saved lineup stays in this browser only.
