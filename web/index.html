Five constructs survived.

Galáctico does not manufacture ratings. Eight candidate measures went through reliability, confound and incremental-information testing across five leagues and a second data provider. Four survived cleanly, one survives conditionally, and three did not. This is what is left.

Volume is not efficiency.

Progression per 90 against progression per completed pass. A player can be high-volume and ordinary-efficiency, or low-volume and elite-efficiency. Collapsing these into one rating would destroy the only interesting thing about the pair.

↖ low volume · high efficiency — selective, used sparingly ↗ high volume · high efficiency — the rare corner ↙ low volume · low efficiency ↘ high volume · low efficiency — high responsibility, ordinary return

Explore a construct.

Distributions across the competition. Quality constructs can be ordered; style constructs cannot, and are not presented as a ranking.

Compare two players.

The difference is the point, not two profiles side by side. Where a gap is smaller than the measurement can separate, it says so.

Why only five?

Every candidate went through the same lifecycle: definition, reliability, confound audit, construct validity, incremental information, robustness. Three failed at three different stages, and the differences matter more than the failures.
