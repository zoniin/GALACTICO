About these numbers
  • Each table is worked out here from the reported results, ordered by points, then goal difference, then goals scored. Official tiebreaks and points deductions can differ.
  • Results come from OpenFootball’s free files, which volunteers update after play. Where a league’s results stop early, the banner says after which matchday the table stands.
  • Bundesliga and Premier League results missing from OpenFootball are filled in from OpenLigaDB, once both clubs and the matchday match. A result OpenFootball already has is never replaced.
  • The Champions League table is its league phase, from OpenLigaDB. Clubs take the name OpenFootball gives them, or the English form.
  • Choose a date to see the table after that day’s results. When a league’s results stop early, the answer above says which matchday the table is after, in the past tense.
