    Under 900 minutes

    These players are in the squad and in no count. Below 900 minutes a rate is not shown and not used. That is the gate, not a judgement.

    About these numbers

    Target sums add up the rates each player recorded at his club, as if he kept them in this XI: an input to the count, not what a team produced. Nothing on this page says which player to keep, sell or sign.

    This cannot say

    A count of eligible players is a count. It does not say the cover is adequate.

    Cover that is thin only after the 900-minute gate is a property of the evidence, not of the squad.

    Eligibility is a declared football rule, not a measurement.
