      About these numbers
      • Fixtures and results come from OpenFootball’s free files, which volunteers update after play, so results can arrive days or weeks late. Each competition shows the date its results reach.
      • Bundesliga and Premier League results missing from OpenFootball are filled in from OpenLigaDB, once both clubs and the matchday match. A result OpenFootball already has is never replaced, and a filled-in result takes the date and kick-off OpenLigaDB gives it.
      • Champions League fixtures, results and scorers come from OpenLigaDB alone. Clubs take the name OpenFootball gives them, or the English form, so a club has one name on every page.
      • Scorers and minutes appear where OpenLigaDB lists them: the Bundesliga, the Premier League and the Champions League. The free La Liga, Serie A and Ligue 1 feeds don’t name scorers.
      • Kick-off times are local times, as each source publishes them. Recent form counts the last five reported results in the same competition.
      • Saved matches stay in this browser only.

      Data: OpenFootball (CC0 1.0) · OpenLigaDB (ODbL 1.0) · API-Football (private, on this computer only).
